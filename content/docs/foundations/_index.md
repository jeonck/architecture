Architecture is not the boxes-and-arrows picture. It is the set of decisions
that are expensive to reverse — and the discipline of noticing which decisions
those are *before* you make them casually.

This section covers the mental model: how to spot an irreversible decision,
how to read the constraints that actually bind you, why coupling is the
currency everything else is priced in, why a boring baseline beats a clever
one almost every time, and which of those prices have moved lately.
