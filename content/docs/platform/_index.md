Every platform decision is a bet on where you want your complexity to live.
Kubernetes does not remove operational burden; it relocates and standardises
it. Infrastructure as code does not stop drift; it gives you a place to detect
it. Knowing what you are actually buying is the whole job.

This section covers what Kubernetes signs you up for, IaC that stays truthful,
environment and secret handling, multi-tenancy and cost boundaries, and running
an internal platform like a product rather than a mandate.
