This is the section the site is named for. Architecture experience is mostly
tacit — it lives in the judgement of people who have been burned. Turning that
into something a new joiner can read is a skill, and most teams are bad at it.

This section covers decision records people actually read, diagrams that stay
true as the system changes, runbooks that work when you are tired and scared,
design reviews that surface disagreement early, and how to build a memory that
does not walk out the door.
