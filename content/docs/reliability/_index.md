Reliability is not an absence of failure. It is the ability to fail in small,
contained, well-understood ways — and to notice quickly when you are not.

This section covers SLOs and error budgets as a negotiating tool, wiring
observability that answers real questions, backpressure and load shedding,
the standard failure-isolation patterns and how they get misapplied, keeping
a system serving when the thing it asks questions of goes away, and incident
response that produces learning instead of blame.
